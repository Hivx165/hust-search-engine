      CREDITS-BASED POLITICAL THEORY CURRICULUM

       

      I.Undergraduate level not specialized in Political Theory

      Students from K64 and later (enrollment from 2019 and later) study 05 modules:

      • Philosophy of Marxism-Leninism

      (SSH1111): 03 credits

      • Political Economics Marxism-Leninism (SSH1121): 02 credits   

      - Scientific Socialism (SSH1131): 02 credits   

      - History of Vietnam Communist Party (SSH1141): 02 credits   

      - Ho Chi Minh Ideology (SSH1151): 02 credits

       

       

      Students from K63 and earlier (enrollment from 2018 and earlier) study 04 modules:

      • Basic Principles of Maxist-Leninism I

      (SSH1110): 02 credits

      • Basic Principles of Maxist-Leninism II

      (SSH1120): 03 credits

      • Revolutionary line of the Vietnamese Communist Party (SSH1130): 03 credits
      • Ho Chi Minh Ideology (SSH1050):

      02 credits

       

       

       

      II. Postgraduate degrees not specialized in political theory:

      Students of engineering, technology and Philosophy: 03 credits

      Students of Economics and Philosophy: 04 credits
