Quang Duc Tran received the M.Sc. degree in Electrical Engineering from Budapest University of Technology and Economics, Budapest, Hungary, in 2008, and the Ph.D. degree in Information Engineering from City University London, London, United Kingdom, in 2014. His research interests include machine learning, image processing, biometric authentication, and various pattern recognition-related algorithms.
