      INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ICPT.HUST 2023 WITH THE PARTICIPATION OF THE CENTRAL PROPAGATION COMMITTEE, INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS, AND A LARGE NUMBER OF DOMESTIC AND FOREIGN RESEARCHERS


      On December 14-15, 2023, the international conference ICPT.HUST 2023 with the topic "Human resources for sustainable development" took place at Hanoi University of Science and Technology. The conference is held in both offline and online.

      Delegates and experts took photos with the organizers of the ICPT.HUST 2023 conference

      The topic of the Conference has important significance, associated with the viewpoint of the 13th Party Congress, identifying human resource development as one of three strategic breakthroughs. At the same time, the Government also issued Resolution 136/NĐ-CP on sustainable development, which emphasizes the view that "people are the center of sustainable development".
      The conference was honored to have the participation of the representatives of the Central Propaganda Department: Mr. Đoàn Văn Báu - Director of the Department of Political Theory, Mr. Phạm Đức Tiến - Deputy Director of the General Department, Mr. Lê Huy Hoàng - Deputy Director of the Department of Education. Besides, Prof. Trần Văn Phòng - a member of the Central Theoretical Council directly discussed and exchanged opinions at the Conference.

      Mr. Đoàn Văn Báu - Director of the Department of Political Theory

      ICPT.HUST 2023 also received the attention of major international agencies and organizations such as Prof. Jonathan Pincus, Senior Economist (UNDP Vietnam), Timothy Ong - USAID HCMC Office Director; Mr. Mitch Kirby - Senior Education Advisor (USAID), Mr. Davide Guarini Gilmartin - Senior Academic Manager, English Programmes (British Council); Ms. Kate Sullivan, Head of Services & Talent, Corporate English Solutions  (British Council). In addition, The conferen also had the participation of Mr. Celso Boene Adelaide - Financial Attaché of the Mozambican Embassy in Vietnam, representing countries with international students studying at HUST. 
      At the same time, participating in the discussion at the Conference were researchers from universities and research institutes in countries with large economies in Asia (such as Japan, China, and Korea) and countries with large industries in Europe (such as Italy, Russia, and Slovakia).

That's Dr. Tran Tuan Phong, Deputy Director of the Institute of Philosophy under the Vietnam Academy of Social Sciences and Assoc. Prof. Hoang Anh, Deputy Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Political Theory under the Ho Chi Minh National Academy of Politics, along with Leaders of the Political Theory Departments of National Economics University, Foreign Trade University, University of Social Sciences and Humanities.

      The 1st session of the International Conference ICPT.HUST 2023

      On the organizing committee's side, there was the presence of Assoc. Prof. Huynh Dang Chinh - Vice President of HUST (Head of the Organizing Committee of the Conference) and leaders of schools, institutes, departments of HUST.

      Assoc. Prof. Huynh Dang Chinh - Vice President of HUST (Head of the Organizing Committee)

      The official sessions of the Conference take place on December 14 and 15, 2023 with 03 topics:

      • The 1st session: Strategies and policies for human resource training for sustainable development

      • The 2nd session: International experiences in developing highly qualified human resources and sustainable development
      • The 3rd session: Orientations and solutions for human resource training and sustainable development for Vietnam

      ​​​​​​At the conference, Assoc.Prof. Huynh Dang Chinh shared: "On behalf of Hanoi University of Science and Technology, I would like to express my sincere thanks to the leaders, managers, and scientists from Vietnamese agencies and organizations and International students who have come to Hanoi University of Science and Technology to attend the ICPT.HUST 2023 International Conference. The opinions discussed at the Conference are extremely useful and respectful. We believe in human resources. high quality and sustainable future development for countries."

      Research and discussions at the Conference provided suggestions on: (1) Public policy for sustainable development; (2) Developing human resources for high-tech industries of the 4.0 Industrial Revolution; (3) English language training meets global labor standards.
      At the same time, many useful international experiences have also been shared by international scholars. In addition, there have been many articles providing useful directions and solutions for Vietnam's sustainable development, linking human resource development with culture and civilized values.

      Assoc. Prof. Luong Dinh Hai spoke in the discussion of the 1st session

      In particular, from an international perspective, there have been extremely positive assessments of Vietnam's economic model and achievements. That is a lesson for many developing countries to create an independent and self-reliant economy in today's world.


      Assoc. Prof. Dinh Thanh Xuan (Dean of Faculty of Political Theory) and Dr.       Tran Tuan Phong
      (Vice Director of Institute of Philosophy)

      After 02 working days, ICPT.HUST 2023 was a great success, creating an open exchange forum with diverse perspectives, from Government agencies and scholars, from international experience to practice. specific practices of Vietnam. Besides, the highlight of success is creating good impressions about culture and people, opening up connections and cooperation in the future.
