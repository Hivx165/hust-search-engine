Service Hours

  1. Reading rooms and Circulation rooms are open during office hours from Monday to Friday. Closed on Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.

      * Morning:  08:00 – 12:00

      * Afternoon: 13:30 – 17:30

Note:

     * The Textbook Check-out Room (Room 111) does not offer borrowing or returning services on Tuesday and Friday mornings for inventory checks and administrative tasks.

      * Self-study rooms 301, 303, 401, and 418 are open outside regular hours: 08:00 – 19:00 (including Saturdays and Sundays).

  1. Extended hours for 4 specialized reading rooms (Room 402, 411, 419, and 526) will be announced in detail on the website during peak exam periods.

     * Monday to Friday: 08:00 – 21:00

     * Saturday, Sunday: 08:00 – 16:00
