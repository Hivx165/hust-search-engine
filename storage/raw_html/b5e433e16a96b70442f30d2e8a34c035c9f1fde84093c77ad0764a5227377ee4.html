      Bachelor-Master of Textile and Clothing technology

      INTEGRATED EDUCATION PROGRAM
      BACHELOR-MASTER OF SCIENCE

       Name of program: Textile and Clothing Technology
      Education level: Bachelor-Master
      Major: Textile and Clothing technology
      Program code: 7520312 (Bachelor) – 8540204 (Master)
      Duration: 5,5 years
      Degree Bachelor in Textile and Clothing technology
      & Master of Science in Textile and Clothing technology
      Credits in total: 180 credits
      Language: Vietnamese

       

      PROGRAM LEARNING OUTCOMES:

      On successful completion of the programme, postgraduate students will be able to:
      1. Apply extensive and advanced theoretical and practical knowledge in the textile and garment industry to analyze, propose and implement optimal solutions for production and management systems in the textile and garment industry.
      2. Synthesize interdisciplinary knowledge and management and administration knowledge to organize, administer and manage research activities and professional expertise, advanced systems/processes in the textile and garment industry.
      3. Propose and implement scientific and practical research topics and projects in the textile and garment sector.
      4. Research, develop and use new technologies creatively in academic and professional fields.
      5. Communicate research-based knowledge, discuss professional and scientific issues clearly and convincingly through a variety of media.
      6. Work effectively in multidisciplinary teams, including collaborating, organizing, and leading to achieve common goals in an international environment.
      7. Proficient in foreign languages (English) in professional work and international communication, reaching a level equivalent to level 4/6 according to the Vietnamese Foreign Language Proficiency Framework.
      8. Self-direct, make expert conclusions, manage, evaluate and improve professional activities; apply critical thinking and lifelong learning to adapt to technological changes.
      9. Demonstrate professional ethics, a high sense of responsibility towards the community and society, and comply with laws and regulations and standards in the textile and garment industry to contribute to sustainable development.
