      Call for papers

      Vietnam Ministry of Education and Training promulgated the document No. 2681/BGDĐT-HTQT dated June 2nd, 2023 approving the organization of the “International Conference on Human Resources for Sustainable Development” at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

      Time for receiving articles: from June 30th, 2023 to September 30th, 2023 on the official website of the Conference icpt.hust.edu.vn. After receiving articles, an independent review process will be implemented to select and publish suitable ones in the Conference Proceedings with ISBN and DOI publication indexes. The official working session of the Conference will take place on December 14th-15th, 2023 in Hanoi, along with the online channel on Microsoft Teams.

      It is an honor for Hanoi University of Science and Technology to invite scientists, managers, and experts to submit papers to the Conference.

      Research directions

      • The relationship between human resource development and sustainable development
      • Education & training and science & technology for enhancing the quality of human resources and sustainable development
      • Developing innovative human resources for sustainable development
      • Culture, ethics with sustainable development
      • High-quality human resources for leadership and management
      • Human resources for digital transformation, green transformation
      • Human development for integration and responding to non-traditional security challenges
      • Human development for an independent and self-reliant economy, as well as national defense and security
      • Other discussion directions related to the conference topic

      Important dates

      • 20/10/2023: Full Paper Submission Deadline
      • 21/10 - 29/12/2023: Double-Blind Review and Full Paper Revision
      • 14-15/12/2023: Conference (Combining onsite and online)
      • 30/12/2023: Abstract or Full Paper Acceptance Notification

      Format rules:

      • The official language of abstracts and full papers: English
      • Summarize no more than 250 words, keywords upto 6 words.
      • The article’s complete text (excluding title, abstract and keywords, tables): 4000 minimum and 10,000 maximum words
      • The article’s entire text is edited in .docx format, with the font of Times New Roman
