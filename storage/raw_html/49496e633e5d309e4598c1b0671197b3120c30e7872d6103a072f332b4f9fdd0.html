Introduction

The online learning management system of Hanoi University of Science and Technology is a software platform that supports teaching and learning activities for lecturers and students. The system effectively leverages information technology applications in education to improve training quality, enhance interaction during the teaching and learning process, and optimize the university’s resources.

Flexible study time

Instructions

Students, lecturers, and staff can access the online Learning Management System (LMS) using their Office 365 accounts provided by Hanoi University of Science and Technology.
To log in, please select the “Login with HUST account” option on the system’s login page.
Courses on the system are available to students enrolled in Blended-learning (Blearning) classes listed on the training management portal.
