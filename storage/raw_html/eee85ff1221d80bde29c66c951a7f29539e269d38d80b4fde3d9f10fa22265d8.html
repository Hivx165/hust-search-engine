About the Conference

QMFS 2026 Conference

A premier international conference bringing together leading researchers, industry experts, and policymakers to explore revolutionary solutions in food quality management and safety.

QMFS 2026 – The 8th International Conference on Quality Management and Food Safety – represents a convergence of cutting-edge science and practical innovation. This premier international conference brings together leading researchers, industry experts, and policymakers to explore revolutionary solutions that integrate food technology, biotechnology, analytical sciences, and quality management across the entire food supply chain.

Join us as we pioneer evidence-based strategies, foster cross-disciplinary collaboration, and shape the future of food systems—where excellence in quality management meets the highest standards of food safety and environmental stewardship.

Our Speakers

Keynote Speakers & Committee

Meet our distinguished speakers and committee members

Prof. Anil Kumar Anal

Food Innovation, Bioprocessing, Nutrition and Health Program of the Asian Institute of Technology

Prof. Anil Kumar Anal is Professor in the Food Innovation, Bioprocessing, Nutrition and Health Program at the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), Thailand, where he currently serves as Chair of the Academic Senate and Dean of the Faculty of Food, Agriculture and Natural Resources. With more than two decades of experience in academic leadership, research, and international advisory services, Prof. Anal specializes in sustainable food systems, circular bioeconomy, food innovation, functional ingredients, food safety, and sustainable nutrition. His research integrates biotechnology, microbiological solutions, nanotechnology, and advanced food-processing technologies to develop resilient and sustainable food systems. Prof. Anal has published more than 200 peer-reviewed publications, books, book chapters, policy papers, and patents, with an H-index of 58 and over 13,850 citations. He has supervised more than 33 PhD and 160 Master's students and has provided expert advisory services to major international organizations, including FAO, ADB, World Bank, WHO, UNEP, UNIDO, and JICA. He also serves as an editorial board member and reviewer for leading international journals and has received multiple awards and fellowships for excellence in research, teaching, and international service.

Prof. Pham Quoc Long

Senior Researcher at IAST - Ton Duc Thang University.

Prof. Pham Quoc Long is a leading expert in organic chemistry, chemistry of natural compounds, and chemical technology. He is currently a Senior Researcher at IAST - Ton Duc Thang University, with more than 250 scientific publications, nearly 100 WoS-indexed papers, and an H-index of 24. His research focuses on lipids, fatty acids, and natural compounds from marine organisms and Vietnamese plants, with applications in pharmaceuticals, functional foods, and biotechnology. Prof. Long has led numerous national-level research projects, holds multiple patents, and has made significant contributions to doctoral training and international scientific collaboration.

Assoc. Prof. Parag Acharya

Leading alternative protein-based food research and innovation at the Natural Resources Institute

Dr. Parag Acharya is a food scientist and innovation leader specializing in alternative proteins, sustainable food systems, food processing, and product innovation. He leads research on food processing and innovation at the Natural Resources Institute (NRI), University of Greenwich, and played a key role in establishing the Medway Food Innovation Centre. His research focuses on developing climate-smart foods from sustainable protein sources, including legumes, algae, and agri-food by-products, through advanced processing, clean-label formulation, and flavour technologies. Dr. Acharya has extensive industry experience from his decade-long career in Unilever Food R&D in the Netherlands, where he led multidisciplinary research and innovation projects and was a lead inventor on four patents. He is actively involved in UK and international initiatives on alternative proteins and sustainable food innovation, working across academia, industry, and policy.

Prof. Toshiaki Komatsu

Global Office – Tokyo University of Marine Science and Technology

Professor Toshiaki Komatsu is a practitioner–scholar specializing in international education, career development, and human resource development. Since joining Tokyo University of Marine Science and Technology in 2012, he has designed and managed international education programs, including overseas internships, field-based learning, and university–industry collaborations across Asia and other regions. He has supported more than 400 students participating in international programs in ten countries and has played a key role in strengthening educational cooperation between Japan and Vietnam, particularly between Tokyo University of Marine Science and Technology and Hanoi University of Science and Technology. His research focuses on how international learning experiences shape students’ global perspectives, learning development, and long-term careers. With approximately 20 years of prior experience in Japanese and American companies, Professor Komatsu brings extensive practical expertise in recruitment, talent management, organizational development, business training, and career development. He is also the author of 16 books on business skills and career development.

Prof. Pham Van Hung

Vice President – International University, Vietnam National University in Ho Chi Minh City

Prof. Dr. Pham Van Hung is an expert in food biochemistry, food science and technology, and functional foods. He received his PhD in Food Biochemistry from Osaka Prefecture University, Japan, in 2005 and has been affiliated with International University, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City (VNU-HCM), since 2009. He currently serves as Vice President of the University and Senior Lecturer in the Department of Food Technology. With more than 25 years of experience in teaching and research, Prof. Hung has led over ten research projects and published more than 100 scientific articles, as well as five books and book chapters. His publications have received over 6,000 citations, with an H-index of 41 (Google Scholar). He received the Ta Quang Buu Award in 2018 and the First Prize of the Ho Chi Minh City Creative Award in 2019. His research focuses on functional foods, natural bioactive compounds, food safety, and the valorization of agricultural and food-processing by-products for sustainable food applications.

Prof. Kohsuke Honda

Director – International Center for Biotechnology, The University of Osaka

Dr. Kohsuke HONDA received his PhD in 2003 from Kyoto University, Japan. After staying there as a post-doctoral fellow, he moved to Department of Biotechnology, Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Osaka (UOsaka), as an assistant professor in 2005. He was appointed as an associate professor of the same department in 2010, and then a full professor of the International Center for Biotechnology, UOsaka, in 2019. His research area is applied microbiology and synthetic biology with a particular interest in the application of extremophiles and their enzymes. He has actively engaged in collaborative research with researchers in ASEAN countries and has co-authored more than 30 of his approximately 130 publications with ASEAN collaborators. He received Amano Enzyme Application Award (2013), and Young Scientist Awards from Japanese Society of Enzyme Engineering (2015), Japan Bioindustry Association (2017), and the Society for Biotechnology, Japan (2018).
