Welcome to PreAPCAT-10 – ISSC 2025

Catalysis for a Sustainable Future: Innovations and Perspectives

July 31 to August 1, 2025 • Hanoi, Vietnam

We are delighted to invite you to join us from July 31st to August 1st, 2025, in Hanoi – the capital of Vietnam for an exciting gathering of leading researchers, industry professionals, and experts in the field of catalysis and sustainable chemistry. The symposium, serves as the Pre-Symposium for the 10th Asia-Pacific Congress on Catalysis (APCAT 10), which will be held from August 4 to 7, 2025, in Singapore.  The symposium, themed “Catalysis for a Sustainable Future: Innovations and Perspectives,” is organized by Hanoi University of Science and Technology in collaboration with the Vietnam Association of Catalysis and Adsorption and RoHan – Rostock-Hanoi SDG DAAD Graduate School.
This symposium provides a unique platform for sharing cutting-edge developments, exchanging innovative ideas, and fostering collaborations across the Asia-Pacific and European regions.
At ISSC 2025, you will have the opportunity to explore groundbreaking advancements in catalysis science and technology, engage in thought-provoking discussions, and network with peers from academia and industry. You will also have the opportunity to connect with different universities in Hanoi and Vietnam as well as the chance to explore a beautiful Hanoi city and surrounding areas.
We look forward to welcoming you to our symposium and to the exciting opportunities at the symposium!

Publication

Accepted abstracts will be published in the conference book of abstracts

Contributors to the oral and poster presentations are invited to submit a manuscript to Vietnam Journal of Catalysis and Adsorption.
Please use the template of ISSC 2025 for your submission (please download JCA-Template at HERE). 
Please submit your paper from the following submission link and select “PreAPCAT-ISSC2025” section.
The submission is open until 30 September 2025.
All manuscripts will undergo peer review in accordance with the journal’s standard editorial process.

Contributors to the oral presentations are also invited to submit a manuscript to Catalysis Surveys from Asia. Please submit your paper from the following submission link:
https://submission.springernature.com/new-submission/10563/3
During submission, please select “Collection on 10th Asia-Pacific Congress on Catalysis (APCAT-10)” from the Collection drop-down menu to ensure your paper is considered for the collection.
The submission is open until 30 September 2025.
All manuscripts will undergo peer review in accordance with the journal’s standard editorial process.
