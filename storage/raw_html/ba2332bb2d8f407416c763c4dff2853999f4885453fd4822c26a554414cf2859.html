Conference Fee

Registration is required for the conference participants with registration fee of 400 USD (regular registration), and 200 USD (for student). Vietnamese affiliation will be applied a reduced price due to the support from organizer. The fee covers the attendance at the conference sessions, conference package, documentation, coffee breaks, lunches and banquet. However, hotel charge and breakfast are not included. Full papers, either for oral or poster presentations, submitted by the deadline and compliant with the paper template have been published in Materials Science Forum or Key Engineering Materials (https://www.scientific.net/) with SCOPUS indexed after peer-reviewed by Scientific board. The additional charge for the publication (80 EURO/paper) will be appreciated.
