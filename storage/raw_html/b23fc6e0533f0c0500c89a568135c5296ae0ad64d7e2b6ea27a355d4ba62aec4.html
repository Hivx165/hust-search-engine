      Training is one of the two key tasks of the School of Chemistry and Life Sciences, Hanoi University of Science and Technology. Continuing to promote the tradition, inheriting the achieved results, the School of Chemistry and Life Sciences - Hanoi University of Science and Technology has launched many policies and solutions to continue to improve the quality of training to meet the increasing requirements of the university. Efforts in training and education have helped HUST maintain and affirm its leading position in training high-quality human resources in engineering and technology, contributing to the implementation implementing the socio-economic development strategies of the Party and the goverment, pioneering in innovation, contributing to the construction and development of the higher education system.
