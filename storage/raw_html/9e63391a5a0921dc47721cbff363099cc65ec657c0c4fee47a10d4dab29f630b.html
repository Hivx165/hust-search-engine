A warm “homecoming”: Chap Canh Foundation awards 55 scholarships at HUST

On the morning of 9 June 2026, Hanoi University of Science and Technology and CC Foundation presented 55 Chap Canh Bach Khoa scholarships for semester II of 2025–2026, worth VND 18 million each, to students who excel despite difficult circumstances.

“Every time I come back to Bach Khoa, I feel like I am coming home.” That heartfelt remark by Mr. Tran Tuan Anh, Executive Director of CC Foundation, captured the spirit of the Chap Canh Bach Khoa Scholarship Award Ceremony for the second semester of the 2025–2026 academic year. Beyond the 55 scholarships presented to students who excel despite hardship, the ceremony was also an occasion to look back on nearly five years of steady companionship between the Chap Canh Foundation and students of Hanoi University of Science and Technology, spreading trust, sharing and the aspiration to rise through knowledge.

Supporting journeys of perseverance

On the morning of 9 June 2026, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, together with Chap Canh Foundation Social Enterprise Co., Ltd. (CC Foundation), held the Chap Canh Bach Khoa Scholarship Award Ceremony for the second semester of the 2025–2026 academic year for 55 students who face difficult circumstances yet keep striving in their studies, achieving academic results of good standing or above and cumulative training scores of fairly good or above. Each scholarship is worth VND 18 million per semester.

Speaking at the ceremony, Assoc. Prof. Dr. Huynh Dang Chinh, Vice President of Hanoi University of Science and Technology, expressed his sincere appreciation and deep thanks to CC Foundation for its constant companionship with the University in supporting students in difficult circumstances.

He stressed that the scholarships presented that day carry not only material value but also precious moral encouragement, strengthening the confidence and the motivation of students to persevere on their educational path.

“I hope you will keep your resolve firm and turn the care and support of the Chap Canh Foundation into motivation to study and train tirelessly, and from there contribute positive values to the community and to society.”

He also expressed his hope that the scholarship recipients would continue to embody the spirit of overcoming hardship and to improve themselves constantly, so as to become responsible citizens and talented engineers and experts in the future.

Nearly five years of sowing trust and opportunity

Since 2021, CC Foundation has become a close companion of students at Hanoi University of Science and Technology. More than 300 students in difficult circumstances have received support through scholarship, training and skills development programmes.

What makes the Chap Canh Foundation distinctive is not only the figures behind its support, but also its philosophy of long term companionship with students. Alongside financial scholarships, the Foundation continuously offers learning and capacity building programmes such as international TOEIC and MOS certifications, CCNA, courses in artificial intelligence (AI) applications, information technology, data analytics and many other skills programmes.

On 28 January 2026, CC Foundation and Hanoi University of Science and Technology signed a further scholarship sponsorship agreement worth up to VND 3 billion. Under the agreement, the Foundation will continue to accompany students of the K67, K68, K69 and K70 cohorts, opening up more opportunities for learning and all round development.

Mr. Tran Tuan Anh, Executive Director of CC Foundation, speaking at the ceremony

Speaking at the ceremony, Mr. Tran Tuan Anh, Executive Director of CC Foundation, said with emotion: “Every time I come back to Bach Khoa, I feel like I am coming home.”

That was not merely a personal sentiment; it also reflected the close and trusting relationship built between the Foundation and the University over the past five years.

Mr. Tran Tuan Anh shared that the greatest happiness for the Foundation is to witness the eagerness to learn that is so characteristic of Bach Khoa students. Their relentless effort, their drive to improve and their determination to rise through knowledge are exactly what has kept the Foundation alongside them over the years. For him, every student success story is not only the student’s own achievement but also meaningful proof of the value of sharing and of trust placed in the right hands.

Sweet fruits nurtured by trust

The joy of those who give opportunity is multiplied when they witness the growth of former scholarship recipients. Among the nine K67 students receiving Chap Canh scholarships this semester, Le Xuan Phuc, a student of the School of Information and Communication Technology, graduated early with Excellent distinction and was commended by the University. That achievement is vivid proof of the value the scholarship programme has brought over the years.

Speaking on behalf of the recipients, Le Xuan Phuc expressed his gratitude to CC Foundation and to the staff of the Student Affairs Department, who have always accompanied and supported students through difficulties on their academic journey. He said: “This scholarship is not only an honour, it is also a motivation for us to keep studying, keep training and live more responsibly.”

Le Xuan Phuc, School of Information and Communication Technology, speaking on behalf of the scholarship recipients

Scholarships today, wings for tomorrow

The ceremony closed in joy and emotion as the scholarship certificates were handed to the students. On behalf of the University and the students benefiting from the programme, Hanoi University of Science and Technology sincerely thanks Chap Canh Foundation Social Enterprise Co., Ltd. for its trust, its companionship and its meaningful contributions to Bach Khoa students over the past years.

The Foundation’s valuable support carries not only material value; it also helps spread a humane spirit and gives many students in difficult circumstances the confidence to pursue their dreams. The University hopes to continue receiving the attention and companionship of CC Foundation in the years ahead, so that more and more students may be given wings, grow and contribute good values to the community and to society.

For beyond the scholarships themselves, what is given today is opportunity, trust and aspiration. These are the very wings that will help Bach Khoa students fly high with confidence on their journey to master the future, and pass the spirit of sharing on to the generations of students who follow.

Author: Truong Thi Thuy Mai
