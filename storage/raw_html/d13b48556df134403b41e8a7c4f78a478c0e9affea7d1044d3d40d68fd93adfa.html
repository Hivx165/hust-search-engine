CONFERENCE FEES

The rate below is calculated for each person per paper.

• Normal registration until 10/10/2026: 200 USD

• Normal registration after 10/10/2026: 250 USD 

• Student registration: 150 USD (certificate proof required)

• VASE member registration: 150 USD (waived for 1 year active membership)

• Group registration: 150 USD (waived for group registration of 5 registration affiliated to the group).

• Plenary speaker/ Invited guest registration: no fee once approved by OB.

• Gala dinner for student registration, spouse/family member in normal registration: 30 USD (free for kids under 12).

• Gala dinner for spouse/family member of keynote/plenary speaker/ invited guest registration: free for 1 each.

• Publication fee: charged separately.


IMPORTANT

The fees include admission to all scientific sessions, the presentation of 1 paper in the conference sessions, conference materials, coffee breaks, and lunches, social events. Each paper to be presented should have at least one registered author whose participation fee has to be paid by 10/10/2026. Full paper contributions presented at the conference will be invited for publication at the Conference Proceedings in LNME series or Special Issues of Selected Journals with an extra charge of 50 USD per paper.
