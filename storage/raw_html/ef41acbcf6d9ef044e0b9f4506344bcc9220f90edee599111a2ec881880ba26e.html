General information

About the programme

The International Capstone Design Program (i-CAPS), also known as the International Students Multidisciplinary Design Camp (d-Camp), is an annual collaborative programme initiated by the Innovation Center for Engineering Education (i-CEE) of Jeonbuk National University, Korea.

2026 Theme

Tech Start-up for Small Businesses

Format

2 stages — Proposal (Feb) → Final (Aug)

Organizer

ICEE, Chungnam National University

i-CAPS is an engineering-based programme in which multidisciplinary student teams collaborate to develop a design proposal and ultimately invent or design a prototype that addresses real community concerns. For the 2026 edition, the theme “Tech Start-up for Small Businesses” focuses on empowering local entrepreneurs and micro-enterprises through creative technology applications and entrepreneurial innovation.

The programme runs in two stages. The first stage, held in February in a partnering country, centres on presenting the capstone design proposal. The second stage takes place in South Korea in August as the final competition and prototype demonstration. Between the two stages, each team completes its prototype over roughly six to eight months.

How the programme is structured

Round 2 · Final Camp

August 2026 · South Korea

i-CAPS final competition and demonstration of completed prototypes.

The 2026 challenge

Theme, scope & objectives

Small businesses are the backbone of local economies, yet often struggle to adopt digital tools, automation and online platforms. Teams blend creativity, business sense and technology to turn ideas into viable start-ups.

Project guidelines

  • Convergent technologies — IoT, sensors, Arduino, 3D printing, SNS, internet apps, drones and more.
  • Outcome — environment-friendly virtual and scaled prototype models.

Team assessment areas

CommunicationTask managementDecision-makingConflict resolutionStudent reflection

Project scope & focus areas

  • Digital Tools for SMEs

    Inventory management, e-commerce, POS or accounting solutions.

  • Mobile Apps

    Apps that connect small vendors with customers or suppliers.

  • Sustainable Business Tech

    Green packaging, eco-friendly production aids or energy tracking.

  • Low-Cost Automation

    Tools that simplify production, packaging or logistics.

  • AI & Data for Small Business

    Predicting trends, managing customers or optimizing sales.

Learning objectives

  • Apply design thinking and engineering principles to develop feasible solutions.
  • Experience cross-cultural teamwork and interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Transform ideas into prototype-level tech start-up concepts.
  • Strengthen pitching, presentation and entrepreneurial communication skills.

Impact

  • Foster creative, innovative thinking and competitiveness for Industry 4.0.
  • Build tolerance and effective communication across cultures and languages.
  • Produce innovative prototypes with commercial potential that benefit the community.

Capstone Proposal Presentation

Guided collaboration to shape a feasible, technology-driven solution for the 2026 theme.

Live and work alongside student teams from six countries across Asia.
