Guideline

General Requirements

  • Each paper must be original, unpublished work and has not been submitted for other publication elsewhere.
  • The authors must not indicate their names, affiliations and acknowledgements in the body of submitted paper.
  • Personal details will be collected separately during the online submission process.
  • All papers will undergo a peer-review process to assess their quality and relevance.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper is required to register and present at the conference.
