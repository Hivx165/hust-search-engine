VENUE

Hanoi, Vietnam
November 05-07, 2026
Location

Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST)

The 9th ASEAN Smart Grid Congress (ASGC 9) will be hosted at the prestigious Hanoi University of Science and Technology campus, located in the heart of Hanoi, Vietnam.

Building: Ta Quang Buu Library
Address: 1 Dai Co Viet Road, Bach Mai, Hai Ba Trung District, Hanoi, Vietnam
Rooms: International Conference Chamber (10th Floor) and Lecture Hall Rooms
Directions

How to get there

HUST is centrally located and easily accessible by various modes of transportation:

  1. From Noi Bai International Airport (HAN):
    It takes approximately 45-60 minutes by taxi or ride-hailing services (Grab) to reach the campus. Alternatively, you can take public Airport Bus No. 86 to the city center and transfer to the campus.
  2. By Taxi / Ride-Hailing:
    Simply search for "Ta Quang Buu Library HUST" or "Hanoi University of Science and Technology" on Grab or tell your taxi driver to go to "Cong Parabol, Dai hoc Bach Khoa Hanoi" (Parabol Gate, Dai Co Viet St).
  3. By Public Bus:
    Several bus routes pass directly by HUST gates on Dai Co Viet Road and Tran Dai Nghia Road, including routes 08, 21, 26, 28, and 31.
