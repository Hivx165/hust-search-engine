Tuan Linh DANG (Đặng Tuấn Linh in Vietnamese) serves as a lecturer at the School of Information and Communications Technology, Hanoi University of Science and Technology. He was conferred with a Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Kochi University of Technology in 2017.

Currently, his research is primarily focused on computer vision and machine learning. Dang Tuan Linh has been the recipient of several prestigious scholarships, including the Special Scholarship Program from KUT for a doctoral program in 2014 and the Japanese government scholarship for a master’s program in 2012.
