1. Registration fees 

Each paper will only be accepted for participation in the Conference (in the form of an oral presentation, full-text publication in the proceedings, or publication in scientific journals recommended by the Conference) if at least one member representing the author group attends. The conference participation fee is described in the table below. 

The conference fee includes: transportation from Hanoi to Ninh Binh and return from Ninh Binh to Hanoi; Welcome dinner on October 22nd; lunch and Gala dinner on October 23rd; lunch on October 24th; tea breaks; and conference materials (proceedings). 

2. Additional Registration Information

When completing the registration form, participants are requested to indicate:

2.1 Transportation Option

- Make your own way to the conference venue.

- Travel via the organizing committee's bus from Hanoi University of Science and Technology to the conference venue (morning of October 22) and back (after 12:00 PM on October 24).

2.2 Optional Activities on October 22, 2026

- Attend the pre-conference workshop "GenAI-Assisted Academic Writing: How to Use AI Without Outsourcing Your Scholarship" by Assoc. Prof. Lee Seong Ju (The Education University of Hong Kong) – 14:00, October 22, 2026.

- International Professional Development Association (IPDA) and Publishing on Practice: Contemporary Issues in Practitioner Education – 15:30, October 22, 2026.

3. Payment Method

3.1. Beneficiary accounts

3.2. For individual payment (for one author/participant):

- Transfer details:

+ Authors who submit their papers via the ICEF website will receive a unique PID code for each paper. Therefore, please include in the transfer details: PID CODE_ICEF 2026.

+ Authors who submit their papers via email, please include in the transfer details: AUTHOR'S NAME_ICEF 2026.

+ Participants who do not submit a paper, please indicate in the transfer details: ATTENDANCEONLY_ICEF 2026.

3.3. For group payments (author groups):

- Each participant must be registered individually on a registration form.

- Please prepare the following information to complete the form:

      + Paper ID(s) (PID), if applicable,

      + Type of participants in your group. There are three main types of participants:

              o HUST faculty/staff – labeled “H”

              o Non-HUST participants – labeled “N”, and

              o Postgraduate and Undergraduate Students – labeled “S”

      + Total amount of payment required

- For each registration, please use the following format in the bank transfer details:

      + If your group has a paper, use the format: PID number [type of participant X] [number of X participants]

Example: “PID300 S2 PID400 H1 PID400 N2” means payment for a group of 5 participants, as follows:

            o Paper with PID300 has 2 student authors (S2)

            o Paper with PID400 has 1 HUST faculty/staff (H1) and 2 regular participants (N2)

      + If registering for attendance only (no paper), please write “ATTENDANCEONLY” instead of a PID number.

Example: “ATTENDANCEONLY H3” means you are making a payment for 03 HUST faculty/staff members to attend the conference without submitting a paper.
