Theoretical and Mathematical Physics (MS)

MASTER PROGRAM

THEORETICAL AND MATHEMATICAL PHYSICS

MAJOR CODE: 8440103

HÌNH THỨC TUYỂN SINH:

  • Xét tuyển.
  • Thi tuyển (Toán cao cấp, Tiếng Anh và Vật lý cơ sở).

MAJOR CODE: 8440103

ADMISSION TYPES:

  • Application Screening
  • Examination (Advanced Math, English và Fundamentals of Physics).

Period : 1,5 YEAR

Tuition Fee: 36 – 37trđ/toàn thời gian học

EDUCATION OBJECTIVES & LEARNING OUTCOMES

Program: Theoretical and Mathematical Physics
Education Level: Master
Major: Theoretical and Mathematical Physics
Major code: 8440103
Education Orientation: Research
Degree: – Master of Science

1. Objectives

General Objectives
a. Training highly qualified human resources, raising people’s intellectual level, conducting scientific and technological research to create knowledge and new products, serving the requirements of socio-economic development, ensuring national defense, security and integration. international.
b. To train learners with political and moral qualities; have knowledge, professional practice skills, scientific and technological research and development capacity commensurate with the training level; have solid professional knowledge, systematic thinking methods; have the ability to detect, analyze and solve problems in the field of Theoretical Physics and Mathematical Physics;

Detailed Objective

At the end of the course, students majoring in Theoretical Physics and Research-oriented Mathematical Physics have the following competencies:

a. Having solid professional knowledge, systematic thinking methods; have the ability to detect, analyze and solve problems in the field of Theoretical Physics and Mathematical Physics; have the ability to raise the level of teaching at the high school and university level; The university knowledge block helps learners to have foundational knowledge, a broad and solid professional base to adapt well to jobs suitable to their fields of study;

b. Having the necessary professional skills and personal qualities, the ability to self-study and improve qualifications for career success, high-level professional knowledge, the ability to independently and creatively do scientific research and can continue to carry out the doctoral thesis immediately.;

c. Have the necessary social skills, the ability to use foreign languages ​​to work effectively in groups and in an international environment;

d. Capable of participating in design, ability to form ideas to design, build, provide technical solutions in research and production.

ADMISSION CRITERIA & DEGREE

Admission is done in the form of:

  • Application Screening:Applicable to candidates applying for research-oriented Master of Science/ Engineering/ Technology training programs (specific requirements for candidates in Appendix 2
  • Examination: The other subjects with three exams are Advanced Math, English and Basic Physics. Candidates can be exempted from the English test, see Clause 3, Article 5 of the Circular promulgating the regulation on training at Master’s level (No. 15/2014/TT-BGDĐT) issued by the Minister of Education and Training. issued on 15/05/2014.
