KEYNOTES

Prof. Jungil Song

Professor Jungil Song is a globally respected expert in composite materials, with over three decades of pioneering contributions spanning natural fiber composites, metal matrix systems, biomedical engineering, and structural reliability. He earned his PhD from Pohang University of Science and Technology and has worked at Changwon University since 1998. He currently serves as Emeritus Professor at Changwon National University (CWNU) and Director of the Center for Advanced Materials Research (CAMR) supported by the National Research Foundation of Korea.

Professor Song's research has profoundly shaped the field of sustainable composite materials, with a strong emphasis on the development of green and bio-based polymers, flame-retardant systems, thermoset bio-resins, self-healing composites, and reinforced natural fibers. His recent work also explores polymer recycling, micro/nano cellulose materials, 3D printing technologies, and advanced polyurethane foams, all aimed at addressing global challenges in materials sustainability and multifunctionality.

Prof. Hiromi Isobe

Prof. Hiromi Isobe is a Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Nagaoka University of Technology (NUT), Japan, and serves as Director of the Manufacturing Workshop Center. He received his doctoral degree in Mechanical Engineering and has been engaged in research and education in advanced manufacturing processes for more than three decades.

His research interests focus on ultrasonic vibration machining, including ultrasonic vibration cutting, drilling, and surface texturing technologies. In recent years, he has been actively investigating functional surface generation through ultrasonic vibration cutting and its tribological characteristics. His group has also developed visualization techniques using high-speed imaging and photoelasticity to clarify dynamic machining phenomena occurring during ultrasonic vibration-assisted processes.

Prof. Isobe has published numerous papers in the fields of precision engineering and manufacturing science and is the author of the book “Ultrasonic Vibration Machining Technology” (Nikkan Kogyo Shimbun). He currently serves as a Delegate of the Japan Society for Precision Engineering (JSPE) and has held several leadership positions in the Japan Society for Abrasive Technology (JSAT), including Chair of the Hokuriku-Shinetsu Branch. He also served as the Chair of ABTEC2024, the annual conference of the Japan Society for Abrasive Technology held in Nagaoka, Japan.

His current research aims to contribute to sustainable manufacturing through advanced machining technologies, including friction reduction and functional surface engineering enabled by ultrasonic vibration machining.

Prof. Jiwang Yan

Prof. Jiwang Yan received his Ph.D. from Tohoku University in 2000 and is currently a professor of mechanical engineering at Keio University, Japan (2012-), leading the Laboratory for Precision Machining and Nano Processing. As adjunct appointments, he has served as a specially appointed professor at the Tokyo Institute of Technology (2017-2024) and a Leverhulme visiting professor at the University of Manchester (2018-2019). His research areas include ultraprecision machining, micro/nano manufacturing, laser processing, nanostructures/materials, and manufacturing sustainability. As a principal investigator, he has led more than 20 nationally funded projects and over 70 joint research projects with industry. He has authored/co-authored 300+ peer-reviewed journal papers, given 150+ keynote/invited talks, and received 40+ awards. He is now executive director of the Japan Society of Precision Engineering and chairman of the publication sector. He serves on the board of the Japan Society of Laser Technology and the editorial boards of several international journals including IJMTM, JMPT, and IJEM. He is a member of JSME, JSPE, JSAP, JSAT, ASPE, SME, euspen, and CIRP, and a fellow of ISNM.

Prof. Kim Yun-Hea

Professor Kim Yun-hea acts as the Co-chair in various international conference series including PHENMA, AMDP. He was the President of the Korean Society for Composite Materials. He published over 120 SCI papers in international journals and 200 papers in national journals. He joined over 60 conferences and symposia at the international and national level as general speaker, invited speaker, keynote speaker and plenary speaker.





Dr. Pham Huy Giao

Dr. Giao currently serves as an Associate Professor in Earth Science of Petrovietnam University (PVU). He received his MSc in Geophysics from Bucharest University, M. Eng. & D. Eng. in Geotechnical Engineering from Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), Bangkok, Thailand. He is a recipient of a few awards such as the Outstanding Academic Performance Award for an overseas Vietnamese student by Ministry of Education and Training (awarded in 1982); The Australian Government Scholarship for a graduate study at the Asian Institute of Technology, The Tan Swan Beng Award of the SE Asian Geotechnical Society (2005); Brain Korea 21 Grant by the Korean Government (1999-2001), the Asia-Pacific lecture tour speaker (2014-2015) by European Association of Geoscientists and Engineers (EAGE), Distinguished Teacher Award 2016 by the Asian Institute of Technology, AIT (2016); Award on Outstanding Contribution in Reviewing by Engineering Geology Journal, Elsevier, 2017; The Trusted Reviewer awarded by Institute of Physics (IOP) for exceptionally high quality review work, IOP, 2020. He is a member of the Awards Committee of EAGE (2021-2027), Editorial Board member for Environmental Geotechnics, Scientific Reports/Springer Nature etc.

Before joining PVU Giao had been a staff, faculty member and Head of the Geotechnical & Earth Resources Engineering field of study, Asian Institute of Technology (AIT) over a few decades, where he could have supervised and graduated more than one hundred graduate students. Notably, Giao founded the famous Geoexploration and Petroleum Geoengineering (GEPG) program at AIT in 2004 and has led it until he relocated to Vietnam in 2020. His research interests include geomaterial characterization, subsurface computation and modeling, exploration and applied geophysics, geotechnical engineering. He is well-known for the studies on FEM groundwater modeling and land subsidence analysis of Bangkok plain and other deltaic plains in the SE Asia; Giao has authored and co-authored more than 200 publications in peer-reviewed international journals and conference papers in geoscience and geoengineering, e.g., on FEM modeling, artificial recharge, soft clay investigation, exploration and applied geophysics, petrophysics, ANN and PINN soft computing, energy transition etc.

Dr. Giao has served as the PI of many large projects of innovative research in geotechnical-geophysical engineering and international education technology, funded by various national and international funding sources. He is a member of major professional societies such as European Association of Geoscientists and Engineers (EAGE), Society of Exploration Geophysics (SEG), International Association for Mathematical Geosciences (IAMG), International Association of Low land Technology (IALT), Society of Petrophysics and Well Log Analysis (SPWLA), International Society of Soil Mechanics & Geotechnical Engineering (ISSMGE), South East Asian Geotechnical Society (SEAGS), Vietnam Association of Geophysicists (VAG), and Vietnam Association of Engineering Geologists and Environment (VAEGE), Vietnam Association of Hydrogeologists (VAH).
