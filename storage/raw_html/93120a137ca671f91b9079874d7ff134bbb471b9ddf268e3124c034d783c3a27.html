      [CH2] Chemistry

       

      ADMISSION CODE

      EDUCATION PROGRAM
      DEPARTMENT OF CHEMISTRY

      TARGET

      ADMISSION METHOD

      CH2

      CHEMISTRY

      160

      - Admission for talented individuals according to the regulations of Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

      - Admission based on the result of the Thinking Skills Assessment (TSA) exam of Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST): K00

      - Admission criteria based on high school graduation exam code: A00, B00, D07, K01

      - Module 1: Organic Chemistry

      - Module 2: Physical Chemistry

      - Module 3: Analytical Chemistry

      - Module 4: Inorganic Chemistry

       

      1. General Introduction:

      The Department of Chemistry is a long-standing traditional training field at the Hanoi University of Science and Technology. This field has applied knowledge of chemistry and related natural sciences to research and develop many practical products for daily life. Choosing the Chemistry Department (CH2) for study, research, and personal development is a career orientation for many students across the country who are passionate about chemistry. Along with the trend of Industry 4.0 development, the Chemistry Department (CH2) is one of the interesting and attractive fields that attracts the attention of many high school graduates every year. With a 4-year undergraduate program and a 4+1.5-year integrated bachelor-master program, students majoring in Chemistry (CH2) at the Faculty of Chemistry and Life Sciences will be a solid human resource for many research and chemical business companies nationwide.

       

      With a quality-assured training program accredited according to AUN-QA standards, students will have the opportunity to choose from four main specialized directions in the Chemistry Department (CH2) that are suitable for their abilities and interests:

      • Inorganic Chemistry

      • Organic Chemistry

      • Analytical Chemistry

      • Physical Chemistry


      2. Information about the education program:

      The curriculum of the CH2 program not only provides students with basic knowledge blocks, foundational and specialized knowledge but also equips them with skills to independently update their knowledge, conduct research independently and creatively, explore practical problems, and apply knowledge of chemistry and creative scientific and technical achievements to solve real-world issues.

      Linked Training Program:

      - The linked training program between Monash University (MONASH) Australia and Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST) follows a 3+1+1.5 structure. Accordingly, students who meet the requirements will complete 3 years out of the 4-year Bachelor of Science program (Chemistry – CH2) at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST) and complete 1 year (the third year) of the Bachelor of Science program majoring in Chemistry at Monash University, Australia. Upon completion of the program, students will be awarded a Bachelor of Science degree majoring in Chemistry from Monash University, Australia, and will have the opportunity to pursue a Master's degree at Monash University, Australia (1.5 years).

      - The dual-degree Master of Science program between ROSTOCK and HUST follows a 1+1 structure. Accordingly, students who meet the requirements and complete the first year of the dual-degree Master of Science program at Hanoi University of Science and Technology will have the opportunity to receive a scholarship for the second year at UR/LIKAT (Rostock), Germany. Upon completion, students will be awarded a Master of Science degree majoring in Chemistry from Hanoi University of Science and Technology and the University of Rostock, Germany.


      3. Scholarship opportunities, study support:

      • Encouragement Scholarship for Academic Excellence.

      • Tran Dai Nghia Scholarship.

      • Scholarships sponsored by domestic enterprises.

      • Scholarships for international student exchange with universities in South Korea, Japan, Thailand, Taiwan, and Europe.

      • Opportunities to receive scholarships for postgraduate studies at Hanoi University of Science and Technology and advanced countries worldwide (USA, Australia, South Korea, Japan, and European countries).

      Additionally, during their study, students will have access to a professional environment, modern laboratory facilities for research, and opportunities for internships in companies relevant to their chosen expertise in the field of chemistry.

       


      4. Job opportunities:

      With the depth of specialized knowledge and carefully equipped skills, graduates after completing the Bachelor's degree in Chemistry (CH2) are well-prepared to adapt to various job opportunities both domestically and internationally, such as:

      - Becoming lecturers and researchers in chemistry at universities such as Hanoi National University, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Phenikka University, Hanoi University of Industry, Mining University, Hanoi University of Water Resources, Hanoi University of Public Health, Hai Phong University of Medicine and Pharmacy, Vietnam Industrial University, Hung Yen University, Vietnam National University of Agriculture, VinUni International University, and some foreign universities.

      - Becoming researchers at major research institutes such as the Institute of Chemistry, Institute of Chemistry of Natural Compounds, Institute of Marine Biochemistry, Institute of Medicinal Materials, Central Drug Testing Institute, Institute of Biotechnology, National Institute for Food Control, Viettel Aerospace Research Institute, Military Medical Forensic Institute, Institute of Building Materials...

      - Becoming specialists at the Intellectual Property Office, Ministry of Health, Ministry of Science and Technology, Ministry of Education...

      - Becoming quality management and assurance specialists, QA/QC, product quality inspectors (KCS), industrial process controllers in companies; Research and development specialists; Analytical testing laboratories, testing rooms, appraisal centers...

      - Becoming managers at factories such as Dung Quat Oil Refinery-Quang Ngai, Nghi Son Oil Refinery-Thanh Hoa, But Son Cement Plant-Ha Nam, Vinasoy Soy Milk Plant-Bac Ninh, Ca Mau Fertilizer Plant.

      - Various other job positions in companies supplying, importing, and exporting equipment, materials & chemicals in the Chemistry industry and Chemical Corporations.
