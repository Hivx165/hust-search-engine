ABOUT BK FINTECH HACKDAY

BK Fintech Hackday is a recurring competition series co-organized by Institute for Digital Technology and Economy and BK Fintech Club, with A-Star Group as a Tech Partner and Finxdemy as a Supporting Partner.

The competition targets university students who are passionate about technology, digital economy, blockchain, and artificial intelligence, and who aspire to develop innovative and practical solutions. It serves as an opportunity for young talents to showcase their creative thinking and problem-solving capabilities, while also acting as a stepping stone to connect with leading technology enterprises.
