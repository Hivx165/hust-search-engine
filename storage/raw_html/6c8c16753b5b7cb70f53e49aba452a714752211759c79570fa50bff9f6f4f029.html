      [CH-E11] Pharmaceutical Chemical Engineering-ELITECH

       

      ADMISSION CODE EDUCATION PROGRAM
      DEPARTMENT OF CHEMICAL ENGINEERING      		 TARGET
      CH1 CHEMICAL ENGINEERING 680
      CH-E11 PHARMACEUTICAL CHEMICAL ENGINEERING - ELITECH  100

      1. General Introduction:

      Pharmaceutical chemistry and pesticide engineering Engineering specialize in the field of isolating, formulating, extracting, and synthesizing bioactive compounds for the production of pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, and plant protection chemicals. Pharmaceutical Chemistry focuses on researching the metabolism, explaining the mechanism of action of compounds at the molecular level, establishing relationships between structure and biological or pharmacological effects.


      2. Information about the education program:

      • General principles of pharmaceutical chemistry and pharmacology.

      • Organic chemistry and methods of organic synthesis.

      • Chemistry of natural compounds and extraction techniques.

      • Technological processes, chemical machinery, and refining techniques.

      • Analytical methods and drug testing.

      • Fragrance and Cosmetic.

      • Formulation techniques.

      • Plant protection chemistry.


      3. Scholarship opportunities, study support:

      • University's merit-based scholarships.
      • Vietchem scholarship for students with difficult circumstances.
      • Students have the option to choose the Bachelor-Master or Bachelor-Engineer transfer program to shorten their study time.
      • The Advanced Pharmaceutical Chemistry program offers a 2+2 partnership program with the University of Adelaide, Australia. Students study for 2 years at Hanoi University of Science and Technology and have the opportunity to transfer to Australia for the final 2 years with scholarships of up to 35%.
      • Many international partnership programs are available for Master's degree students, such as the 1+1 Master of Chemistry program with the University of Rostock - Germany or Monash University, University of Adelaide - Australia.

      4. Job opportunities:

      • Bachelor’s and engineers operate, design, and improve production lines for pharmaceutical and plant protection products such as pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, functional foods, veterinary drugs, and plant protection chemicals.
      • Lab analysts, testing raw materials & products of pharmaceuticals and plant protection.
      • Researchers at research and product development departments, specialized research institutes.
      • Lecturers at universities, colleges teaching subjects and majors related to pharmaceuticals and plant protection.
      • Production management, commercialization of pharmaceutical and plant protection products.
      • Business, customer care, and warranty of pharmaceutical products, medical equipment, and manufacturing.
