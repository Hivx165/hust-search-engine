Electronic Document Directory by Course

Introduction:
 The Electronic Document Directory by Course is a bibliographic information product that consists of a catalog of electronic materials, organized according to the training courses of the Schools and Institutes at Hanoi University of Science and Technology. These materials are sourced from reputable electronic databases that the university currently has access to.
