      INVITATION LETTER OF THE INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ICPT.HUST 2023

      ICPT

      Vietnam Ministry of Education and Training promulgated the document No. 2681/BGDĐT-HTQT dated June 2nd, 2023 on approving the organization of the “International Conference on Human resources for sustainable development” at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).
      Hanoi University of Science and Technology respectfully invites you to attend the Conference, details as follows:

      • Time:          From 08:20 a.m, December 14, 2023

      • Venue:      Room 222, Building C1, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, No.1 Dai Co Viet Road, Hai Ba Trung District, Hanoi

      • Audience: Central Propaganda and Training Commission, Ministry of Planning and Investment of Vietnam, Ministry of Education and Training of Vietnam, Ho Chi Minh National Academy of Politics, Board of Directors of universities, Vietnamese and international researchers…

      We are pleased to welcome delegates to the Conference.
      Link to instructions to join the conference by online: HERE
      Thanks and best regards.
