Short Bio

Phi Le Nguyen obtained her Bachelor of Engineering and Master of Science degrees from the University of Tokyo in 2007 and 2010, respectively. She joined HUST in 2010 and returned to Japan in 2016 to pursue her Ph.D. In 2019, she earned her doctorate in Informatics from the National Institute of Informatics, Japan.
She currently serves as the Director of the Institute for AI Innovation and Societal Impact (AI4LIFE) and holds the position of Associate Professor at the School of Information and Communication Technology, Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST). She leads a large research group of talented and ambitious students, focusing on both theoretical AI and its applications in environmental science, climate change, and smart healthcare.
With a portfolio of more than 150 articles published in esteemed journals and conferences such as ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, EMNLP, and IJCAI, Dr. Nguyen has received multiple best paper awards, including those at ISSNIP 2014, ICT-DM 2019, CCGrid 2023, and CANDAR 2023. Her research has been funded by several renowned institutions, including VinIF, NAFOSTED, the Ministry of Education and Training (MOET), the Ministry of Science and Technology (MOST), and Aus4Innovation.
She actively contributes to leading conferences as a Technical Program Committee member and serves as a reviewer for top-tier journals and conferences, including AAAI, ICLR, NeurIPS, CVPR, ECCV, UAI, ICCV, IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking (ToN), and the IEEE Internet of Things Journal (IoTJ). She serve as an Associate Editor of ACM Computing Surveys.
