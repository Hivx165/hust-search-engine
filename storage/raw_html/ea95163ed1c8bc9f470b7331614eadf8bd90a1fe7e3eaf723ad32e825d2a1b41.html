About me!

Huynh Thi Thanh Binh is Professor and Vice Dean of the School of Information and Communication Technology (SoICT), Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST). She is Head of the Optimization Group at SoICT.

She is a Chair of the scientific committee of Computer Science, The National Foundation for Science and Technology Development - NAFOSTED (2025, 2027).

She is Associate Editor of Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence Journal (EAAI), The IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in Computational Intelligence (TETCI); Swarm and Evolutionary Computation (SWEVO).

Her current research interests include Computational Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, Memetic Computing, Evolutionary Multitasking.
