Submission

All of the abstracts and papers must be submitted via ICHST-2027’s EasyChair system. The abstract is within the limit of 300 words and should be used as the template Abstract Template ICHST-2027 (.docx) or (.pdf). Full accepted papers will be published in the ICHST-2027 Conference Proceedings and submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore®.

Instructions for Authors (Full-text Paper submission)

All authors should prepare full versions of papers in Microsoft Word (DOC) or Portable Document Format (PDF). Papers must be limited to six pages, including text, references, tables and figures. The paper should follow the IEEE standard template – Download.
