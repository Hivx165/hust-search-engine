INSTRUCTION FOR POSTER PRESENTERS

Poster Preparation

Presenters registered their contribution as poster presentations should prepare and print their poster.

- Poster size: Width 83 cm × Height 113 cm.

- Printing type: Glossy decal

All posters should be hanged in the designated area in the poster hall under their corresponding PIDs before 10:00 (AM) Friday 06/11/2026.

Posters will be evaluated during the poster session for the BEST POSTER PRIZE. The prizes will be presented to authors during the Gala Dinner of the Conference.

Support
