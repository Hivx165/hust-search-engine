THE CITY OF DA NANG

Ba Ria - Vung Tau, a vibrant coastal province in Southern Vietnam, offers a compelling mix of relaxing beaches and profound cultural heritage. Its provincial capital, Vung Tau City, is a major highlight, easily accessible from Ho Chi Minh City. The province is famed for its beautiful coastline, featuring popular spots like Bai Sau and the quieter Long Hai Beach and Ho Tram. For stunning panoramic views, visitors flock to the towering Christ of Vung Tau Statue on Little Mountain and the historic Vung Tau Lighthouse. Adventure seekers can explore the lush scenery and hot springs of Binh Chau National Forest. The islands of Con Dao, a popular destination for diving and nature, offer pristine beaches and rich biodiversity. Ba Ria - Vung Tau is rich with spiritual sites and historical landmarks. Key attractions include the colonial-era Bach Dinh, a former French residence offering a glimpse into the past, and numerous pagodas such as Thich Ca Phat Dai. Visitors can also experience local traditions by visiting the Thang Tam Communal House which showcases the unique culture of coastal fishermen, or explore the historical significance of the Con Dao Prison complex.
