Sensor Lab

  • Khoa Tự động hóa · Thành lập 2023
  • Trưởng phòng: PGS.TS Nguyễn Quốc Cường
  • The Sensor Laboratory is a research lab under SEEE-HUST. The lab focuses on research related to Smart Sensors and their applications in various fields such as Smart Homes, Smart Cities, Precision Agriculture, and Smart Factories.
