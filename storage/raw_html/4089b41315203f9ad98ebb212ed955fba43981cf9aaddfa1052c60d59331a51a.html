      SCIENTIFIC SEMINAR: THE CRISIS OF DEMOCRACY IN EUROPE

      Ngô Quế Lân

      At 2:30 p.m. on Thursday, July 6, 2023, the Faculty of Political Theory held a Scientific seminar with the topic The Crisis of Democracy in Europe at the meeting room of Faculty 312B-D3 with speaker Prof.Marco Brunazzo of the University of Trento (Italy).

      At 2:30 p.m. on Thursday, July 6, 2023, the Faculty of Political Theory held a Scientific seminar with the topic The Crisis of Democracy in Europe at the meeting room of Faculty 312B-D3 with speaker Prof.Marco Brunazzo of the University of Trento (Italy).

      The topic of the seminar attracted the attention of many teachers in the Faculty, students who loved political theory subjects, including foreign students studying at Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

      Speaker Prof.Marco Brunazzo exchanged many contents related to the Democracy Crisis in Europe

      Assoc. Prof.Dr. Dinh Thanh Xuan - Dean of the Faculty gave flowers to thank Prof.Marco Brunazzo for exchanging with teachers and and students.

      After more than 2 hours of work, the seminar ended with the regret of the faculty and staff of the Faculty and the students.

      The seminar brought a lot of new knowledge to the teachers and students. Thank you Prof.Marco Brunazzo for coming to the seminar of the Faculty of Political Theory.
