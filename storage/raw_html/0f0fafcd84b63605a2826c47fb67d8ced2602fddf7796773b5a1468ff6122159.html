About ASGC9

The 9th ASEAN Smart Grid Congress (ASGC9) is a prestigious regional scientific and technological forum in ASEAN, bringing together scientists, experts, policymakers, businesses, and international organizations working in the fields of power systems, energy, and smart technologies.

The congress aims to facilitate the exchange of ideas and experiences in researching and developing micro smart grid technologies at both national and ASEAN levels and to strengthen international collaborative networks in academia, research and the development of smart grid technologies and renewable energy for the electrical grid systems of the ASEAN region.
