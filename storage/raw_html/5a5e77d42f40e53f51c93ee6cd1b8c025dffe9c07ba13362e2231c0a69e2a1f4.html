Admission methods

Talent-based admission XTTN Assessment of aptitude and achievements; priority for international certificates (SAT, ACT, A-Level, AP, IB) and academic-excellence awards.
Thinking Assessment Test ĐGTD Admission based on the results of the Thinking Assessment Test organised by HUST.
High-school graduation exam THPT Admission based on subject-combination scores from the 2026 national high-school graduation exam.

FAQ

What admission methods does SEEE use?

SEEE follows HUST’s three methods: talent-based admission (XTTN), the Thinking Assessment Test (ĐGTD), and the high-school graduation exam.

What fields does SEEE offer?

Four fields: Electrical Engineering, Control Engineering & Automation, Electronics & Telecommunications, and Biomedical Engineering, with programs ranging from standard and advanced (English-taught) to talent and international joint programs.

How do advanced programs differ from standard programs?

Advanced programs are taught in English, aim for international accreditation (AUN-QA, ASIIN, CTI) and strengthen international integration; standard programs are taught in Vietnamese.

What about tuition and scholarships?

Tuition follows HUST regulations each year and differs between standard and advanced programs. Students have many merit-based and corporate scholarship opportunities. Contact the Admissions Office for specific figures.
