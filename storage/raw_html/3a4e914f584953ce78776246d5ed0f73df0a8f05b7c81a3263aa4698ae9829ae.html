SEEE brings together an experienced body of lecturers and scientists, many of whom are leading experts in electrical engineering, automation, electronics & telecommunications and biomedical engineering. With 62 professors and associate professors and 166 PhDs, its proportion of doctoral-level staff is among the highest in the country.

Nearly 70% of PhDs were trained abroad — in France, Japan, Germany, Australia, South Korea, the United States and many other countries — bringing back advanced research directions and a broad international cooperation network. The team both teaches and leads numerous research projects, international publications and inventions, linking education with practice and technology transfer.
