      Hanoi University of Science and Technology announced the establishment of 2 more schools

      admin

      This morning (June 7, 2023), Hanoi University of Science and Technology held a ceremony to announce the founding resolutions and decisions to appoint the management positions of the School of Materials, School of Chemistry and Life Sciences.
      School of Materials and School of Chemistry and Life Sciences are the next two schools established by Hanoi University of Science and Technology after the Minister of Education and Training recognized the University Council, the President of the University Council and the University Council. University director. This is a new step for Hanoi University of Science and Technology in the process of implementing the development strategy for the period 2017-2025 and implementing the thematic Resolution No. 85/NQ-DU dated December 7, 2020 of the Party. Committee of Hanoi University of Science and Technology on transforming the organizational model and building an autonomous and modern Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

      Thus, together with 3 Schools: Mechanical, Electrical - Electronics, Information and Communication Technology, Hanoi University of Science and Technology currently has 5 schools, established under the Law on Amending and Supplementing a Number of Articles of the Law. Higher education (Law No. 34/2018). In the coming time, the organizational structure and operating apparatus of Hanoi University of Science and Technology will continue to be optimized in the direction of leanness, effectiveness and efficiency; strongly decentralize activities for specialized units; centralized management and administration according to the strategy and general rules and regulations issued by Hanoi University of Science and Technology, learners are awarded a unique degree from Hanoi University of Science and Technology.
      The School of Materials provides specialized training to meet the human resource needs of all industrial fields

      School of Materials - Hanoi University of Science and Technology - was established on the basis of merging the following units: Institute of Materials Science and Technology; Institute of Textiles - Footwear and Fashion; International Training Institute in Materials Science (ITIMS), Research Center for Polymer and Composite Materials and Department of Printing Technology.

      GS. Huynh Trung Hai – School of Research Management – was appointed Principal of the School of Materials.

      The Vice Rectors include: Assoc. Le Thai Hung, Assoc. Nguyen Thanh Liem, Prof. Nguyen Duc Hoa.

      Speaking at the ceremony, Prof. Le Anh Tuan - Chairman of the Council of Hanoi University of Science and Technology - analyzed the school's name as "Materials" which is quite easy to understand in terms of content and shows strong interdisciplinary character. Materials science, materials engineering and the application of materials to serve the economy, society and national security are the important goals of training and research of the School of Materials. Materials are the foundation of all branches, technical fields, and technology of Hanoi University of Science and Technology, materials are also products and processes directly applied to practice. The School of Materials trains undergraduate and graduate majors related to traditional materials and new materials, meeting the human resource needs of all industrial fields, such as: engineering , advanced materials, materials and nanostructure fabrication technology from source materials to smelting, forming processing, non-ferrous alloys and surface treatment; electronic materials science, microelectronic engineering and nanotechnology; optical, optoelectronic and photonic materials; textile technology - footwear and fashion; polymeric materials - polymeric-based composites, printing and communication techniques.

      The professional qualifications of the lecturers of the School of Materials are shown through impressive numbers: 93.65% of the School's lecturers are PhDs, nearly 40% of the lecturers have the titles of Professor, Associate Professor.

      The main research subjects of the School of Materials - Hanoi University of Science and Technology - include: Metallic and non-metallic materials, electronic and photonic materials, composite materials, biomedical materials, and energy materials. quality, nanomaterials... with typical research directions: physical, chemical, mechanical... of materials; Simulation of materials calculation; Computational simulation of technological processes; Research and develop advanced materials systems, smart materials, and environmentally friendly materials; Research, application and development of source technology; Research and apply high technology to life; Decoding technology, consulting technology.(Xem chi tiết về Trường Vật liệu TẠI ĐÂY).
      Trường Hóa và Khoa học sự sống đào tạo những ngành thuộc lĩnh vực khoa học, công nghệ và kỹ thuật chủ đạo, mang tính bền vững, mở rộng cơ hội việc làm cho sinh viên

      Trường Hóa và Khoa học sự sống – Đại học Bách khoa Hà Nội được thành lập trên cơ sở sáp nhập 4 Viện: Viện Kỹ thuật hóa học, Viện Công nghệ Sinh học & Công nghệ Thực phẩm, Viện Khoa học và Công nghệ môi trường, Viện Nghiên cứu và Phát triển ứng dụng các hợp chất thiên nhiên. Trường có đội ngũ giảng viên và cán bộ được đào tạo bài bản, có trình độ cao với trên 90% giảng viên có học vị tiến sĩ và gần 40% giảng viên có học hàm giáo sư, phó giáo sư.

      Tại buổi lễ, GS. Lê Anh Tuấn rất tự hào cho biết tên Trường là Hóa và Khoa học sự sống thể hiện rõ sự kế thừa nền tảng truyền thống: Hóa học – Sinh học – Thực phẩm – Môi trường của Đại học Bách khoa Hà Nội, vừa thể hiện khát vọng đáp ứng ngày càng tốt hơn nhu cầu của thực tiễn, mang hơi thở của thời đại và sẵn sàng chinh phục những lĩnh vực mới phục vụ cho định hướng phát triển xanh, phát triển bền vững. “Khoa học sự sống – Life Sciences” là một khái niệm khá mới đối với chúng ta nhưng nó là khái niệm khá phổ biến, là chủ đề rộng, thu hút sự quan tâm của nhiều đại học, viện nghiên cứu trên thế giới.

      PGS. Chu Kỳ Sơn - Viện trưởng Viện Công nghệ Sinh học và Công nghệ Thực phẩm được bổ nhiệm làm Hiệu trưởng Trường Hóa và Khoa học sự sống.

      Các Phó Hiệu trưởng gồm: PGS. La Thế Vinh, PGS. Nguyễn Hồng Liên, PGS. Nguyễn Thị Ánh Tuyết. Trường Hóa và Khoa học sự sống đào tạo các ngành Hóa học, Kỹ thuật hóa học, Kỹ thuật hóa dược, Kỹ thuật sinh học, Kỹ thuật thực phẩm, Kỹ thuật môi trường, Quản lý tài nguyên và môi trường. Đây là những ngành vốn gần nhau về kiến thức cơ sở ngành, thuộc lĩnh vực khoa học, công nghệ và kỹ thuật chủ đạo, mang tính bền vững. Trường có các trung tâm, các PTN nghiên cứu đồng bộ hiện đại kết hợp với phương pháp giảng dạy tiên tiến, qua đó sẽ mở rộng cơ hội việc làm cho sinh viên sau khi tốt nghiệp, đa dạng hóa nguồn nhân lực chất lượng cao cho sự phát triển của đất nước trong tình hình mới. (Xem chi tiết Trường Hóa và Khoa học sự sống TẠI ĐÂY)

      Quyết tâm cao trước chặng đường mới

      Tại buổi lễ, phát biểu nhận nhiệm vụ, PGS. Chu Kỳ Sơn – Hiệu trưởng đầu tiên của Trường Hóa và Khoa học sự sống, Đại học Bách khoa Hà Nội – đã nêu quyết tâm Trường Hóa và Khoa học sự sống cần phát huy tối đa các thế mạnh và thành tựu mà 4 đơn vị đã đạt được trong 67 năm trong đào tạo, NCKH, đổi mới sáng tạo, chuyển giao công nghệ và hợp tác đối ngoại.
      Before all leaders of Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Assoc. Chu Ky Son promises that together with the school leaders and units in the school will focus their wisdom, energy and experience; promote the spirit of UNIVERSITY - UNITY - COOPERATION - DEVELOPMENT to lead, direct and operate all aspects of the school's activities in the coming time in order to create a professional, dynamic and creative environment, where each teacher, each staff member and each student has the opportunity to show their ability and promote their creativity, where each member's comments and suggestions for the development of the University are appreciated. recognition so that we have a dynamic, professional working environment and have real joy at work, where teachers are the subject, the driving force for development, and the students are the center
      And GS. Huynh Trung Hai - the first Principal of the School of Materials - expressed: To be able to achieve success, complete the assigned tasks, and meet the set expectations, first and foremost, it is necessary to must have the consensus and contribution of all staff of the School of Materials. We believe that the staff of the School of Materials will be a unified block; the same goal; make efforts and maximize the capacity of each individual; respecting differences in view of harmonizing interests and adhering to common principles.

      For current and future students of the School of Materials, Prof. Huynh Trung Hai sincerely shared: The school always puts the children at the center of all activities. We will bring all our wisdom, enthusiasm and responsibility to support the children to develop their best abilities and qualities; realize the full potential of each individual. Through training, research, corporate cooperation and other support activities, we aim to fully and balanced equip students with: In-depth, up-to-date theory advanced technology, technology in production and practical experience. That will be the premise for them to develop their career, achieve success and happiness in life. On behalf of the leaders of Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Prof. Le Anh Tuan - President of the University Council - congratulated the 2 Schools of Chemistry & Life Sciences and the School of Materials, the 2 Rectors and the Vice Rectors on receiving the appointment decision today, and expressed their gratitude to the students. believes and hopes for the development of the two Schools of Chemistry and Life Sciences and the School of Materials, as well as the determination of the two Rectors shown in the two acceptance speeches.

      “Difficulty and challenges in the early stage of establishment are inevitable, given the large staff and students, the organizational structure is lean, requiring professionalism in operation, determination and determination. , unanimity and solidarity; requires consistency and synchronization in all activities. When the management and operating system is not really complete, it is necessary to have a high degree of determination and solidarity. Experiences from 3 schools of Mechanical, Electrical - Electronics and ICT are valuable lessons for the two newly established schools" - Prof. Le Anh Tuan emphasized.

      With the strong motivation of Hanoi University of Science and Technology, with the high determination of the leaders of the two universities, the solidarity and consensus of all officials and employees; With the students' empathy, trust and enthusiasm, the 2 newly established schools will soon stabilize, overcome the immediate difficulties and successfully implement the annual plan, towards the strategic goals that have been set. in the project of building the university, contributing to the successful implementation of the development strategy of Hanoi University of Science and Technology until 2025, with a vision to 2030.
       
      In March 2023, at the announcement of the decision to transform Hanoi University of Science and Technology into Hanoi University of Science and Technology and awarding decisions on recognition of the University Council, the President of the University Council, the University Rector, and the University Council. In the 2020-2025 period, Minister of Education and Training Nguyen Kim Son emphasized: "The transformation of the model from university to university is not and is not a trend, but it must be viewed as a tool to unleash creativity. from within, the solution to creative capacities by a new mechanism. Polytechnic University needs to take the lead in implementing autonomy. The university is fully autonomous, has a depth of autonomy, and is autonomous in a new period, both according to the world's practices and in accordance with the political, social, economic, human and cultural characteristics of the university. Vietnam".

      The establishment of 5 schools of Hanoi University of Science and Technology is one of the steps to perfect the university structure with a strict structure, modern management and administration system; perfecting the long-term development strategy and orientation; perfecting the regulations on organization and operation… Towards a university model - an appropriate model for implementing a multidisciplinary structure - under the direction of the Government leaders, the leaders of the Ministry of Education and Training.
