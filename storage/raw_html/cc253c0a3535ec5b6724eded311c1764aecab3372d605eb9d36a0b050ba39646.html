      Board of Presidency


      1.    President: Prof. Le Anh Tuan
      Email: tuan.leanh@hust.edu.vn 

      The University President is responsible for the comprehensive leadership and management of all activities within the functions, duties, and powers of the University as stipulated in the Law on Higher Education, the Regulations on the Organization and Operation of Universities, and other relevant legal documents.

      In charge of Department of Administration; Department of Organization and Personnel; Department of Finance and Planning.

      2. Vice President: Assoc. Prof. Huynh Quyet Thang
      Email: thang.huynhquyet@hust.edu.vn

      In charge of Department of Science and Technology (Promoting cooperation with Hanoi City in the field of science and technology); Department of Organization and Personnel (Regarding emulation and reward activities); Department of Investment Promotion and Infrastructure Development (campus in Hoa Lac); Department of Administration (Party affairs); Faculty of Political Theory; BK Fintech; Institute of Research and Application of Artificial Intelligence; University Trade Union; Veterans Association.

      3. Vice President: Assoc. Prof. Tran Ngoc Khiem 
      Email: khiem.tranngoc@hust.edu.vn 

      In charge of Department of Facilities; Department of Investment Promotion and Infrastructure Development; Department of Inspection, Legal Affairs and Internal Audit (Legal and internal audit work); School of Materials Science and Engineering; Faculty of Engineering Physics; Faculty of Education.

      4. Vice President: Prof. Vu Van Yem
      Email: yem.vuvan@hust.edu.vn 

      In charge of Department of Academic Affairs; Department of Admissions; Center of Continuing Education; Center for Communications and Digital Knowledge (Communications work); Department of Investment Promotion and Infrastructure Development (Hung Yen branch); BK-Holdings; School of Electronics and Electrical Engineering; School of Economics and Management; Faculty of Foreign Languages.

      5. Vice President: Assoc. Prof. Nguyen Phong Dien
      Email: dien.nguyenphong@hust.edu.vn 

      In charge of Department of Partnership and Engagement; Center for Communications and Digital Knowledge (Library and JST work); Department of Inspection, Legal Affairs and Internal Audit (Inspection work); Department of Quality Management; Bach khoa Service Center; Bach khoa Medical Center; School of Mechanical Engineering; Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics.

      6. Vice President: Assoc. Prof. Huynh Dang Chinh
      Email: chinh.huynhdang@hust.edu.vn 

      In charge of Department of Student Affairs; Department of Partnership and Engagement (Business Cooperation work); Center for Foreign Languages Assessment and Culture Exchange; Bach khoa Publishing House; School of Chemistry and Life Sciences; Faculty of National Defense - Security Education; Faculty of Physical Education; Institute of Health Science and Technology; Youth Union, Student Association.

      7. Vice President: Assoc. Prof. Ta Hai Tung
      Email: tung.tahai@hust.edu.vn

      In charge of Department of Science and Technology; Center of Digital Transformation; Center for Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship; Institute of Automation and Control Technology; Institute of Space and Underwater Technology; Institute of Energy Technology; School of Information and Communication Technology.
