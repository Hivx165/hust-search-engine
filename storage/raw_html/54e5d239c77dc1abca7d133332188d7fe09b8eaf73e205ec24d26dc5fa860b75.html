NCT Lab

Welcome to NCT Lab

Networking and Communication Laboratory at School of Information and Communication Technology, or short NCT Lab, consists researchers and lecturers of Department of Communication and Computer Networks.

Every year, many students conduct their research and their final dissertations in NCT Laboratory in the scope of scientific projects led by researcher staff of Department of Communication and Computer Network.

Our research interests include:

▪ Advanced Network Technologies

▪ Network and Information Security

▪ Multimedia Communication Technologies

▪ Inter-connection of different wireless networks of smart devices

▪ Inter-connection of different wireless networks of smart devices (wireless sensor and actuator networks, vehicular networks, M2M communication networks, WiFi-based devices, cyber physical system) to produce the real-world data.
