      What did Hanoi Polytechnic teachers say that made talented students nationwide… “fall in love”?

      admin
      2023-07-17T14:24:03+07:00 2023-07-17T14:24:03+07:00 https://cdt.hust.edu.vn/en/news/news-articles/what-did-hanoi-polytechnic-teachers-say-that-made-talented-students-nationwide-fall-in-love-5.html https://cdt.hust.edu.vn/uploads/cdt/news/2023_07/20230604-cbo_6880.jpg
      Trung tâm Chuyển đổi số https://cdt.hust.edu.vn/uploads/cdt/logo-dhbk-1-02_130_191.png
      Sunday - 04/06/2023 14:21
      •  
      •  
      Today (June 4, 2023), Hanoi University of Science and Technology held an interview with nearly 3,000 candidates applying to Hanoi Polytechnic at 292 councils in the form of Talent Selection (XTTN) category 1.3. This year, the school held interviews 100% online, creating favorable conditions for talented students across the country to have the opportunity to apply to Hanoi University of Science and Technology.
      On June 1, 2023, the Admissions Council of Hanoi University of Science and Technology announced the enrollment scheme for 2023. Accordingly, the total enrollment target for the regular undergraduate system is 7,985 students, of which 15-20 % for talent selection method, 85-90% for admission based on test scores (High School Graduation and Thinking Assessment).

      In the XTTN method, there are 3 categories of admission: Direct admission according to the regulations of the Ministry of Education and Training; Admission for international certificates SAT, ACT, A-Level, AP, IB; Selection by profile and interview. All for the benefit of the candidates

      The atmosphere at Hanoi University of Science and Technology on the weekend was quiet and peaceful. Many students eagerly entered the online interview room early! "Postponing" the students' joy of talking with the Polytechnic, the teacher made an appointment with them: "At 8 o'clock, I'll see you!". On time, in the council rooms, teachers - students exchanged online in a very happy and comfortable mood.

      According to Assoc. Nguyen Phong Dien – Deputy Director of Hanoi University of Science and Technology, interviewing is a very important and indispensable step in the talent selection process to select students to consider their psychological and psychological preparation. , to what extent is the determination to achieve the goal of entering Hanoi University of Science and Technology; Along with that is the style, exchange and presentation of students.

      “After a very long time of impact of the Covid-19 epidemic, the use of online tools with teachers and students is now very familiar. The online interview format will help candidates avoid having to travel, especially those in remote areas. In addition, online interviews also help students have a more stable and confident mentality when sitting at home, using MS tools. Teams to discuss with teachers. Therefore, Hanoi University of Science and Technology decided that 100% of the councils use the form of online interview” – Assoc. Nguyen Phong Dien shared. Nguyen Phu Trong - an English major, Nguyen Du High School for the Gifted, Dak Lak - is delighted to have the opportunity to apply to Hanoi University of Science and Technology under XTTN 1.3. Having a special passion for engineering, Phu Trong boldly applied to a school rich in tradition, ranked in the top of Vietnam and international rankings. “I like to study and experience at the same time with training programs at Hanoi University of Science and Technology, especially after completing my studies, I have a job!” – Nguyen Phu Trong confided in the interviewing teachers/teachers.

      From Buon Ma Thuot to Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Nguyen Phu Trong did not consider the long geographical distance to be difficult. “What's important is what's good for your bright future. I accept the challenges of being away from home, difficult subjects like Calculus, Algebra in general… if I can get into Hanoi University of Science and Technology.” The Southern student expressed his determination. As for Pham Van Tra - a student at Ha Tinh High School for the Gifted - whose father is a wounded soldier, his mother is a math teacher - freely shared with teacher Vu Hong Thai, teacher Nguyen Tai Vuong and Ms. Phung Xuan Lan about why he was "single heart" towards Hanoi University of Science and Technology. That is besides the quality of training and professional qualifications that are among the best in the country of Bach Khoa, Tra is also a big fan of the university's male student football team - the team that she cheers enthusiastically in the Football Tournament. national students in 2022. "This is also one of the goals I strive to study at Hanoi University of Science and Technology" - Ha Tinh student honestly shared.

      Pham Van Tra determined that if he could study at Hanoi University of Science and Technology, major in Computer Engineering, after graduating he would found a software company. After finishing the interview, unlike the friends who said goodbye immediately, Tra smiled brightly and asked to take pictures through the computer screen with the Bach Khoa teachers!

      2 female students named Trang "fall in love" with Bach Khoa since childhood

      Students applying to Hanoi University of Science and Technology under XTTN are very confident in themselves. In particular, many children have connections with Bach Khoa from their own relatives, love this school since childhood. So that when I grow up, even though I specialize in a different subject than Math, Physics, and Chemistry, I still have one heart towards science and technology.​​​​​​​ Do Mai Thu Trang is a student in class 12A1 of High School for Gifted Students of Natural Sciences, University of Science, Vietnam National University, Hanoi. At the top of the Biology class, Do Mai Thu Trang still wants to try in the field of science and technology of Hanoi Polytechnic. This passion also goes into choosing science movies, sci-fi movies to "gnaw" in his spare time.

      Thu Trang said that her sister used to take the entrance exam to Hanoi University of Science and Technology, so since she was in junior high school, Trang had heard her talk about Polytechnic a lot, and naturally had the intention to conquer this school. Later, Trang researched and found herself more suitable for Hanoi Polytechnic. So even though most of the students in high school are oriented towards pharmacy, Trang is still determined to pursue her dream of studying science and technology.

      To conquer her dream, Trang was determined to study to reach the top of her class and school. Trang also works as a tutor, works part-time at a store to accumulate money to pay for 5 years if she can study at Bach Khoa. The female student confidently confided to the interviewing teachers: “I found out about the tuition fees of the major I want to study. I see that I can personally pay for it, my family will only support it!”. And Nguyen Thi Quynh Trang - a student in the Biology class, Vinh Phuc High School for the Gifted during 3 years of high school loves to learn about communication trends between electronic devices and users.

      When researching schools with majors in electronics and computer science, Trang "rated" Hanoi University of Science and Technology. Trang's father - a former Polytechnic student, majoring in Mathematics and Information Technology - provided her with additional information about Polytechnic from the experiences of a person who studied for 5 years at Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

      When answering the interview, Trang showed a very deep understanding of Hanoi Polytechnic, and even had very convincing arguments when proving that women are capable and determined to pursue a scientific discipline. technicians at Bach Khoa.

      To prepare for today's interview, Trang has selected information that is sealed in 10 pages and presented daily. Trang wishes that during her studies at Hanoi University of Science and Technology, she can experience and practice at foreign companies and multinational companies in science and technology. Information provided by the interviewer: Right from the 4th year, 90% of our students have practiced and worked at foreign companies - making Trang very excited!
      Candidates after the interview: The teachers are so chill, I want to study Polytechnic!

      Immediately after the end of the interview round, on the page of Hanoi University of Science and Technology or on social networking groups about Admissions to Hanoi University of Science and Technology, K68 - Hanoi University of Science and Technology... The students' lovely Gen Z language about Polytechnic teachers/teachers, even some friends jokingly said: Sticking to such charms, you have to "swing" Encyclopedia!

      Tai Khuong account: "The teachers are so cute. The more I want to enter the Polytechnic!”. And Duy Ninh's account wrote: "The teachers are very chill, stay calm 😊". Two friends Trinh Xuan Hung and Vu Khanh Vy exchange: The teachers are funny and comfortable / Teachers are cute!...

      From Thai Nguyen, Hue May's parent shared: "Two years ago I was also very nervous, my son also auditioned for talent, and now he is a second-year student of the School of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, very proud. always!".

      Dang Tuyet's account thanked the university page: "The teachers are wonderful and friendly. Sincere thanks to Hanoi University of Science and Technology”…
      The students also shared very close words of encouragement written on Facebook before the interview with Mr. Assoc. Huynh Quyet Thang – Director of Hanoi University of Science and Technology:

      “…Seeing the teachers at the Talent Interviewing and Selection Councils discussing in groups. A high sense of responsibility, heard that a teacher this afternoon brought his son to his grandmother's house to "peacefully work" tomorrow.

      Interviews were conducted online as in previous years. The teachers gathered under the Councils at the School. Candidates conduct online interviews at home.

      Modern, respectful, responsible.

      Think of the interview as a natural exchange of information. There is nothing to stress or worry about!

      Wish the 2K5 students success, express themselves, choose the right industry. Along with that, the image of teacher Do Thanh Dung - School of Materials, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, walking on crutches to the panel to interview candidates for talent selection 1.3 also "caused a storm" on social networking sites.

      Modern, civilized in communication, loving, respecting students, responsible for assigned tasks... is the image of Hanoi University of Science and Technology teachers in the hearts of nearly 3,000 talented students. participated in the interview with XTTN area 1.3 today.
       
      2929: Number of candidates XTTN (category 1.3) participating in the online interview round;
      292: Number of panel interviewing candidates online;
      15 minutes: Maximum interview time is 1 candidate;
      June 8, 2023: Announcement of XTTN results.
