      SCLS Youth Union

      •   30/09/2026 10:05:50
      •   Viewed: 51
      •   Feedback: 0
      The Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union of the School of Chemistry and Life Sciences, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, brings together dynamic, creative and enthusiastic Youth Union members and young people. Guided by the spirit of “Dynamism – Creativity – Dedication – Growth,” the School’s Youth Union supports students in their studies, scientific research, voluntary activities, and cultural and sporting pursuits. It provides opportunities for students to connect with one another, develop their individual potential, strengthen their sense of responsibility, and cultivate a commitment to serving the community
