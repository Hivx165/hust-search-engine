      Postgraduate Training

      Participate in training and supporting Ph.D training: The precursor of the Institute - CNC Center, in particular, the participating units in general, all have a lot of experience in Ph.D. training. The Institute actively participates in making changes to improve the quality of doctoral education. More Specifically: Creating suitable conditions for postgraduate students to associate research topics with scientific study and practical needs, as well as to associate graduated students with the institute’s scientific activities. 
       
      Some suggested research directions for postgraduate: 

      • Research issues related to renewable energy systems. 

      • Electric drive. 

      • The smart system. 

      • AI in smart agriculture 

      • Improve the performance of magnetic bearing motors.

      • Industrial communication - Delay and control issues in real time.
