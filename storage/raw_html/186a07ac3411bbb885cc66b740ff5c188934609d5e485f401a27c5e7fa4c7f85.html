Mission

Developing people, training high-quality human resources, doing scientific research, creating technology and transferring knowledge in the field of Mathematics and Informatics, serving the development of society, nation building and international integration.

Vision

To become a leading prestigious training and research organization in Vietnam and the region in the field of Mathematics and Informatics. Pioneering in scientific activities of Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

Core Values

 

  • Excellence and Effectiveness

The cornerstone of the Faculty’s reputation lies in its commitment to outstanding quality and optimal efficiency in all activities and across all dimensions.

  • Dedication and Commitment

Passion and dedication are the keys to success. Generations of faculty members and students at the Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics are defined by their whole-hearted devotion and enduring contributions.

  • Integrity and Respect

Academic and professional integrity, along with respect for human dignity, the law, institutional regulations, diversity, and differences, are core ethical standards upheld in our academic environment.

  • Individual Talent and Collective Intelligence

Breakthrough success stems from individual creativity and talent, but sustainable achievements are built on solidarity and collective intelligence.

  • Heritage and Innovation

Sustainable development is driven by innovation rooted in the legacy of human knowledge, the continuation of past accomplishments, and the promotion of valuable academic traditions.
