SECOND CALL FOR PAPERS

Dear Distinguished Scientist,

Thank you for your support and contributions in MMMS Conference Series. The first MMMS event organized in 2018 has obtained great success with almost 500 participants from 10 countries and resulted in 130 proceedings publications and 70 international journal publications. Since then, the conference series has been organized biannually in 2020, 2022, 2024 with a growing reputation and attracted great attention from international scientists, leaders and industrial partners worldwide, bringing together almost 800 Scopus-indexed papers so far. More on the conference history can be found here.

The organizers of the conference wish to have your continued support and warmly welcome your participation in the next conference - MMMS2026. The event is hosted by the Vietnam Association for Science Editing (VASE) in collaboration with Petrovietnam University (PVU), Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), Kyungpook National University (KNU-Korea), Nagaoka University of Technology (NUT-Japan), Vietnam Federation of Mechanical Engineering Associations (VFMEA) and Council of Asian Science Editors (CASE).

Important milestone of MMMS2026

- Conference Time: 5-8 November 2026

- Conference Place: Ba Ria – Vung Tau

- Abstract submission: 16/03/2026

- Full paper submissions: 15/06/2026

Please kindly promote this conference within your network actively. Your support is highly appreciated.

Receiving strong supports from scientists worldwide, the MMMS organizers have updated the conference committees with more renown scientists.
