ICECH2026

14th International Conference on Emerging Challenges:
NAVIGATING BUSINESS UNCERTAINTY AND TRANSFORMATION

21 – 22 August, 2026

Da Nang, Vietnam

The University of Danang – University of Economics, Hanoi University of Science and Technology – School of Economics and Management, International School – Vietnam National University Hanoi, University of Economics and Business – Vietnam National University, National Economics University – Faculty of Business Management, University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City, Foreign Trade University, University of Economics – Hue University, University of Hertfordshire (UK), AVSE Global (France), PPM School of Management (Indonesia), EM Normandie Business School (France), and University of Macerata (Italy) will organize the 14th International Conference on Emerging Challenges: Navigating Business Uncertainty And Transformation in Da Nang, Vietnam on August 21 -22, 2026. We would like to invite you to be a part of the ICECH2026 and submit your research papers for presentation. The details are as follows:

The contemporary corporate landscape is increasingly defined by unprecedented volatility and structural shifts, necessitating a profound re-evaluation of established management paradigms. The ICECH2026 international conference seeks to interrogate the complex interplay between organisational resilience and strategic evolution—the emerging patterns of leadership, operational agility, and sustainable growth—within an era increasingly shaped by systemic uncertainty and rapid transformation.

Central to this discourse is the imperative for enterprises to not merely withstand but flourish amidst turbulence. Submissions exploring the mechanisms of organisational metamorphosis, strategies for navigating geopolitical and economic instability, and the role of adaptive leadership in fostering long-term resilience are particularly encouraged. Furthermore, the transformative potential of digital integration and sustainable innovation remains at the heart of these strategic shifts. From the implementation of decentralised autonomous systems to the adoption of circular economy models, new frameworks are redefining how value is synthesised and delivered. This conference invites papers that examine the strategic navigation of these transitions, the inherent challenges of leading through ambiguity, and the ethical considerations of large-scale corporate transformation.

We welcome submissions that delve into the core catalysts of change, exploring how organisations might leverage data-driven insights and human-centric strategies to secure a sustainable competitive advantage. The theme “Navigating Business Uncertainty and Transformation” underscores the critical requirement for firms to balance short-term agility with long-term vision, ensuring robust development in a global economy that remains in constant flux. The ICECH2026 aims to provide a premier forum for academics and practitioners to share multi-disciplinary research, innovative frameworks, and empirical findings regarding the craft and implementation of resilient strategies. While maintaining a global outlook, the ICECH2026 Conference will focus particularly on (but not be limited to) the dynamics within Asia-Pacific nations. We welcome submissions in the following areas: Economics, Business Law, Management, Finance and Banking, Innovation and Technology, Accounting and Auditing.

Authors of accepted papers will be invited to present their work at the conference. In addition, authors of the best papers will also be invited to submit their papers to a special issue or a regular issue for publication consideration in journals affiliated with the conference. These papers will also be subject to an official, rigorous, double-blind peer-review process by the journal. This conference will be organized on the following tracks and topics:

1. Innovation, Technology, and Economics

2. Leadership, Strategic Management, and Business Law

3. Supply Chain Management, E-commerce, and Marketing

4. Sustainable development and Inclusive Societies, Blockchain and AI

5. Quantitative and Resilient Finance

6. Accounting and ESG Disclosure

7. Operation and Management Information System

8. Industry best practices in business transformation

In addition to the main tracks, the ICECH2026 will host a Doctoral Colloquium, specifically designed to foster academic exchange and provide doctoral students with expert feedback on their thesis development.

Keynote Speakers


Professor Liu Zhenya 

Professor Liu Zhenya is currently a distinguished professor at EM Normandie Business School in France and at the School of Finance, Renmin University of China in Beijing. He previously held positions at the International Monetary Fund headquarters, JP Morgan Futures, and the University of Birmingham. Professor Liu has conducted extensive research in financial econometrics and hedge funds. He is recognized as a pioneer, innovator, and practitioner in hedge fund research and practice in China. He established one of China’s first MSc programs in quantitative investment at the School of Finance, Renmin University of China, and assisted Winton Capital, the world’s largest futures hedge fund, in launching its first CTA product in China. He has published nearly 100 papers in internationally renowned journals, including the Journal of Econometrics, Econometric Theory, and the Journal of Business and Economic Statistics, as well as over 20 books.

 

Professor Hussain Rammal 

Professor Hussain Rammal is a professor of International Business and Management at Adelaide University, where he also serves as Academic Lead for International and External Engagement in the School of Management. Currently President of the Academy of International Business’s Oceania Chapter, he brings extensive research leadership through roles as Editor of the Review of International Business and Strategy journal, and as Associate Editor of the Journal of Knowledge Management and the Business & Society journal.

Hussain’s research has been published in leading business and management journals and focuses on contemporary business areas, including sustainable global supply chains, service-sector internationalization, knowledge transfer across multinational networks, global talent management, and cross-border business negotiations.

 

Professor Sudha Mani

Associate Professor Sudha Mani is an Associate Professor of Marketing at Monash Business School, Monash University (Australia) and serves as Co-Editor of Industrial Marketing Management. Her research focuses on the governance of inter-organizational relationships, including franchising, strategic alliances, and buyer–supplier relationships, with a strong emphasis on applying causal inference methods to address managerially relevant business-to-business (B2B) challenges. Her work has been published in leading journals such as the Journal of Marketing, Journal of Marketing Research, Strategic Management Journal, and Industrial Marketing Management. She also serves on the Editorial Review Boards of the Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science and the Journal of Retailing. Beyond academia, her research has informed franchising policy in Australia, including the establishment of the Franchise Disclosure Register, and she previously served on the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission’s Small Business and Franchising Consultative Committee. She is the recipient of the 2021 Dean’s Research Impact Award and the Vice-Chancellor’s Award for Diversity and Inclusion (2019 and 2022).
